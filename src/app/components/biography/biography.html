Biography

Emmanuel is a Mexican director and cinematographer working across fashion, film and visual storytelling.

His work combines directing, cinematography and production, with a focus on visual atmosphere, movement, light and cinematic composition.

He has directed and worked on productions across Mexico, the United States, Europe and Asia, collaborating with brands including:

ALEXANDER WANG - NIKE - MOWALOLA - JNBY - SHEIN - COEVAL MAGAZINE - VOGUE.
